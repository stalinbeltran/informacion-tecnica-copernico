Audience

Dirigido a arquitectos, desarrolladores, integradores, jefes de proyecto, socios externos y responsables de seguridad y cumplimiento.

pp. 5

The intended audience for this document includes system architects, software developers, system integrators, project managers, and other stakeholders involved in the development, deployment, and maintenance of the CopernicusLAC platform. It is also relevant for external partners and third-party service providers who need to understand how to integrate their systems with the CopernicusLAC platform, as well as for security and compliance officers responsible for ensuring that external interactions meet regulatory requirements.

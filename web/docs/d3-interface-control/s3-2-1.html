Data Formats

La entrada estándar del ETL es un STAC Item; sus assets deben venir en GeoTIFF, COG u otros formatos acordados.

pp. 14

The ingestion process expects a STAC Item describing the data which is the standard input for the ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes. The STAC Items assets should be in formats such as GeoTIFF, Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF (COG), or other user-defined standards,

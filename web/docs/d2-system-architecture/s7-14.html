PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL: base de datos relacional para los metadatos STAC, prevista como servicio gestionado del proveedor de nube.

pp. 76

PostgreSQL is a powerful, open-source relational database management system (RDBMS) utilised by the CopernicusLAC platform for storing and managing metadata, particularly in relation to the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC). PostgreSQL is known for its robustness, scalability, and support for complex queries, making it an ideal choice for handling the large volumes of metadata generated by EO data processing tasks.

In the context of the CopernicusLAC platform, PostgreSQL is expected to be provided as a managed service by the cloud provider. This approach offers several advantages, including automated maintenance, backups, and scaling, allowing the platform to focus on its core functionalities rather than database management. By leveraging PostgreSQL as a service, the platform benefits from the cloud provider's expertise in ensuring high availability, security, and performance, further enhancing the reliability and efficiency of metadata storage and retrieval.

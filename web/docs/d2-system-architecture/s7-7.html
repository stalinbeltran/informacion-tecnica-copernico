Argo Workflows

Argo Workflows: motor de workflows nativo de contenedores para orquestar trabajos paralelos en Kubernetes.

pp. 74

Argo Workflows is an open-source container-native workflow engine for orchestrating parallel jobs on Kubernetes. Within the CopernicusLAC platform, Argo Workflows is used to define, schedule, and execute complex workflows that involve multiple steps and dependencies. By leveraging Kubernetes' scalability and Argo's workflow management capabilities, the platform can process large EO datasets, automate repetitive tasks, and ensure that workflows are executed reliably in a distributed environment.

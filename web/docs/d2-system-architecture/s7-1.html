Purpose

Justificar la selección tecnológica, describir su implementación e integración, y facilitar la transferencia de conocimiento.

pp. 72

The purpose of detailing the technologies and frameworks used in the CopernicusLAC platform is to provide a clear understanding of the technical foundation that supports the platform's functionality. This section aims to justify the selection of specific technologies based on their capabilities, describe how they are implemented, and explain their integration within the platform. Additionally, it enhances transparency for stakeholders, supports development and maintenance efforts, and facilitates knowledge transfer by offering detailed documentation on the technologies used. By doing so, this section ensures that the platform adheres to best practices and operates reliably, and securely.

Background

CopernicusLAC es una colaboración entre ESA, DG-INTPA y el Gobierno de Panamá para crear un Centro Regional Copernicus enfocado en reducción del riesgo de desastres, dentro de la Alianza Digital UE-LAC y la estrategia Global Gateway.

The CopernicusLAC initiative is a collaborative effort between the European Space Agency (ESA), the European Commission's Directorate-General for International Partnerships (DG-INTPA), and the Government of Panama. The initiative aims to establish a Regional Copernicus Centre in Panama to facilitate the uptake and utilisation of Copernicus data in the LAC region. The primary focus of the Centre is to support disaster risk reduction and recovery efforts, leveraging Earth Observation (EO) data and geospatial technology to provide information services to local and regional stakeholders.

This initiative is part of the broader EU-LAC Digital Alliance, which promotes cooperation in digital and space technologies between the European Union (EU) and LAC countries. The CopernicusLAC Centre is envisioned as a pilot project under the EU's Global Gateway strategy, aiming to foster international collaboration, enhance data sharing, and support the development of local capacities in EO data processing and utilisation.

The CopernicusLAC Centre is a regional facility dedicated to the management, processing, and dissemination of EO data from the Copernicus Program, specifically tailored for the LAC region. The centre aims to provide comprehensive services for data hosting, processing, and access, supporting a wide range of applications, including environmental monitoring, disaster risk reduction, and sustainable development. The centre also focuses on enhancing local capacities by transferring knowledge and providing training to local operators.

The CopernicusLAC Centre Platform refers to the technical infrastructure and software environment that enables the functionalities of the CopernicusLAC Centre. This platform includes the hardware, software, middleware, and network components required to ingest, process, store, and distribute Copernicus EO data. The platform supports various workflows and tools necessary for data management and analysis, ensuring access and usability for different stakeholders. It is designed to be scalable and capable of handling large volumes of data and supporting a growing number of users and applications.

Based on stakeholder and user engagement, several EO-based Information will be co-designed focusing on 1) Risk and Recovery mapping, 2) Spatially explicit vulnerability and preparedness assessment for Disaster Risk Reduction on a national to regional level,

including baseline maps, and 3) EO monitoring of extreme weather and climate-related events (e.g. heatwaves, extreme rainfall, droughts, flooding and wildfires, etc.)

The Platform introduces tools and services, leveraging open EO data and geospatial technology, to perform the pre-operational demonstration of the Information Services and to deliver and transfer validated products to selected mandated stakeholders.

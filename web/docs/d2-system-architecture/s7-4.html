Kubernetes

Kubernetes: orquestación de contenedores, escalado, balanceo y alta disponibilidad.

pp. 73

Kubernetes is a powerful container orchestration platform used by the CopernicusLAC platform to manage the deployment, scaling, and operation of containerized applications. Kubernetes automates the deployment process, ensuring that containers are distributed across the available infrastructure, balancing the load and maximising resource utilisation. This technology is responsible for maintaining high availability and resilience in the platform, as it can automatically restart or relocate containers if they fail, ensuring uninterrupted service.

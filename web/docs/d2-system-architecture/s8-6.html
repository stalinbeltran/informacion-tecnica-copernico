Incident Response

Plan de respuesta a incidentes en cuatro fases: detección, contención y mitigación, recuperación, y notificación al DPO de la ESA y a los interesados.

pp. 79–80

The platform has a comprehensive incident response plan to manage and mitigate the impact of security incidents:.

  • Incident Detection: The platform uses automated monitoring tools to detect potential security incidents in real-time. These tools are configured to alert the security team immediately when anomalies or breaches are detected.
  • Containment and Mitigation: Upon detection of an incident, the security team follows predefined procedures to contain the breach and mitigate its impact. This may involve isolating affected systems, revoking access privileges, or deploying security patches.
  • Recovery: The platform has protocols in place for restoring normal operations following an incident. This includes data recovery processes, system restoration from backups, and thorough testing to ensure that the issue has been resolved.
  • Notification and Reporting: In compliance with the ESA PDP Framework, any incidents involving personal data are reported to the ESA DPO and, where required, to the affected data subjects. Detailed incident reports are also prepared to document the event, the response actions taken, and any lessons learned.

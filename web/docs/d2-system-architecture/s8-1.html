Purpose

Objetivo: proteger los datos —en especial los personales— y cumplir el marco de protección de datos personales de la ESA (PDP).

pp. 77

The purpose of this section is to outline the security and compliance objectives within the CopernicusLAC platform. Ensuring the protection of data, particularly personal data, and adhering to regulatory requirements is key to maintaining the integrity, trust, and operational effectiveness of the platform. This section will detail the strategies and practices employed to safeguard data, manage access, and respond to security incidents, all while complying with relevant legal and organisational standards, including the ESA Personal Data Protection Framework (PDP).

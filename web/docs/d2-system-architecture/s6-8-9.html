Testing and Validation

SHOULD: pruebas unitarias, de integración y benchmarks antes del despliegue, con datasets de prueba.

pp. 71

Before deployment, applications SHOULD undergo thorough testing and validation. This includes unit tests, integration tests, and performance benchmarks. Developers should provide test datasets and ensure that their applications are fully validated within the platform’s CI/CD pipeline.

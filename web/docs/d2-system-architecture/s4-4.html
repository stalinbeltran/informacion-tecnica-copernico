Data Validation and Content Validation

Validaciones específicas por nivel: presencia y conformidad del STAC Item, presencia de assets, verificación de checksums, validación de formato y comprobaciones de calibración y transformación.

pp. 43–44

After the application package has been selected, the data undergoes a content validation step tailored to the chosen ingestion level. This ensures the integrity, compliance, and usability of the ingested data, whether it involves metadata-only registration, dataset copying, or dataset calibration. The validation process dynamically adjusts to the ingestion level to ensure efficient handling of data requirements.

For metadata-only ingestion workflows, validation focuses on the compliance and completeness of metadata records. Key steps include:

  • STAC Item Presence: The dataset must include a valid STAC Item at the root level of the metadata archive. This ensures the metadata conforms to the cataloging standards required for discovery.
  • STAC Item Validation: The STAC Item is checked for compliance with the STAC specification, including mandatory fields (e.g., id, type, bbox, datetime) and valid data formats. Deviations are flagged for manual review to maintain the integrity of the metadata catalog.

For workflows involving dataset copying, validation extends to the content of the datasets themselves, ensuring their readiness for storage and access. Key steps include:

  • STAC Asset Presence: The metadata must reference all associated data files, ensuring that the dataset includes all necessary components. Missing references result in the dataset being flagged for further review.
  • Checksum Verification: The platform calculates and verifies the checksums of all referenced assets to ensure data integrity. Any inconsistencies trigger an error, and the dataset is moved to temporary storage for manual inspection.
  • Format Validation: Datasets are checked to confirm that they adhere to supported formats (e.g., GeoTIFF, COG, or .zip), ensuring compatibility with downstream workflows.

For workflows involving dataset calibration, validation includes additional checks and transformations to ensure that the datasets meet predefined analytical or quality requirements:

  • Calibration-Specific Checks: The content is evaluated for parameters relevant to the calibration workflow, such as spatial resolution, temporal alignment, or sensor-specific attributes. Any deviations from the required specifications are flagged for adjustment or operator intervention.
  • Transformation Validation: After calibration processes such as georeferencing, re-projection, or resampling, the datasets are validated to confirm that the transformations have been applied correctly and do not introduce errors.
  • Enhanced Metadata Validation: The metadata is updated to reflect the calibration processes applied, ensuring traceability and compatibility with analytical workflows.

For all ingestion levels, the platform ensures compliance with overarching standards and best practices:

  • STAC Item Validation: Regardless of ingestion level, the STAC Item is validated for mandatory fields, appropriate links to data assets, and correct formatting. This guarantees interoperability with the platform's catalog and discovery tools.
  • Error Handling: If any validation step fails, the platform logs the error and isolates the dataset or metadata in temporary storage. Depending on the issue, the system may retry the process, flag the dataset for manual inspection, or alert system administrators for resolution.

System Objectives

Cinco objetivos: accesibilidad a los datos, capacidad de procesamiento, federación con el Centro de Datos de Chile, escalabilidad y soporte/formación a instituciones locales.

pp. 9

The primary objectives of the Platform are:

  • Data Accessibility: Facilitate easy access to Copernicus data for users in the LAC region.
  • Data Processing: Provide data processing capabilities to transform raw satellite data into usable information for various applications such as disaster risk reduction and recovery.
  • Federation: Ensure data exchange and interoperability with the Copernicus Data Centre in Chile and other relevant entities.
  • Scalability and Flexibility: Design a system that is scalable and flexible to accommodate future growth and additional functionalities.
  • User Support and Training: Support local institutions by providing training and tools necessary for the independent operation of the CopernicusLAC Centre.

The CopernicusLAC Centre implements the tools and activities for:

  • Data hosting: Store and provide access to Copernicus Sentinel data and services to the LAC region.
  • Data processing: Development of the middleware needed to process Sentinel and other relevant data.
  • Data exchange: Federation with the Copernicus Data Centre in Chile, and with other relevant entities.

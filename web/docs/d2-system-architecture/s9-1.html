D2 · System Architecture Description · Capítulo 9.1

Purpose

Objetivo: garantizar que la plataforma escale ante cargas crecientes sin comprometer velocidad, fiabilidad ni eficiencia de recursos.

pp. 81

The purpose of this section is to define the goals and objectives related to scalability and performance within the CopernicusLAC platform. Ensuring that the platform can scale to handle increasing workloads and maintain optimal performance under varying conditions is critical to its success. This section outlines the strategies, optimizations, and processes implemented to achieve these goals, ensuring that the platform can support growing user demands and complex data processing tasks without compromising on speed, reliability, or resource efficiency.

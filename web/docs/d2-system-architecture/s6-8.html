Compliance with Platform Standards

Nueve estándares que los desarrolladores deben cumplir al empaquetar sus algoritmos EO, expresados con MUST y SHOULD.

9 apartadospp. 69–71

To ensure integration and reliable operation of EO applications within the CopernicusLAC platform, developers must adhere to specific platform standards during the application packaging process. Compliance with these standards is essential for maintaining consistency, security, and performance across the platform’s various components. This subsection outlines the key standards and practices that developers must follow when packaging their EO algorithms.

Apartados

6.8.1

Use of Common Workflow Language (CWL)

MUST: toda aplicación EO debe describirse con CWL para garantizar portabilidad y reproducibilidad.

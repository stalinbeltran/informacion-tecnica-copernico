D2 · System Architecture Description · Capítulo 3.8.1

Purpose

Automatizar el aprovisionamiento y escalado de los recursos de infraestructura, reduciendo la carga operativa.

pp. 36

The Resource Management component of the CopernicusLAC platform is designed to provision, manage, and scale the infrastructure resources required by the platform’s various components. This includes managing cloud resources, ensuring optimal utilisation, and maintaining high availability and reliability of services. The primary purpose of this component is to automate and streamline resource management tasks, reducing operational overhead and enabling the platform to scale dynamically based on demand.

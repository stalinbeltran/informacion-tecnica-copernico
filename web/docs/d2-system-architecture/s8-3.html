Data Protection and Privacy

Cumplimiento del ESA PDP: cláusulas contractuales tipo de la UE para transferencias, minimización de datos, gestión del consentimiento y derechos de los interesados.

pp. 77–78

Data protection and privacy are of paramount importance in the CopernicusLAC platform, particularly given its compliance with the ESA Personal Data Protection Framework (PDP). The platform adheres to stringent data protection principles, including data minimization, purpose limitation, and ensuring the rights of data subjects:

  • ESA PDP Compliance: The platform complies with the ESA PDP Framework, which governs the protection of personal data within ESA's operations. This includes adherence to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for the Transfer of Personal Data to third countries, ensuring that any data transfers outside the EU are authorised by the ESA Data Protection Officer (DPO) and subject to adequate safeguards.
  • Data Minimization: Only the minimum amount of personal data necessary for the operation of the platform is collected and processed. This principle helps to reduce the risk associated with data breaches and ensures compliance with privacy regulations.
  • User Consent Management: The platform includes mechanisms for obtaining and managing user consent for data processing activities. Users are informed about how their data will be used, and they have the ability to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Data Subject Rights: The platform is designed to facilitate the exercise of data subject rights, such as access, rectification, erasure, and data portability, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

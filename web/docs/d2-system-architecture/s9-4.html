Load Testing and Benchmarking

Pruebas de carga periódicas, benchmarking contra métricas predefinidas y monitorización continua con Prometheus.

pp. 82

To ensure that the CopernicusLAC platform meets its performance requirements, rigorous load testing and benchmarking are conducted:

  • Load Testing: The platform undergoes regular load testing to simulate high-traffic scenarios and assess how well it handles increased demand. These tests help identify potential bottlenecks and ensure that the platform can maintain performance under stress.
  • Benchmarking: Benchmarking is used to measure the platform’s performance against predefined metrics, such as response times, throughput, and resource utilisation. These benchmarks provide a baseline for performance and are used to track improvements or identify areas that need optimization.
  • Continuous Monitoring: The platform integrates monitoring tools like Prometheus to continuously track performance metrics. This allows for real-time analysis of system performance and early detection of potential issues, enabling proactive optimization efforts.

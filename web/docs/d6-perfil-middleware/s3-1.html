PERFIL · Comparación de perfil y definición del rol · Capítulo 3.1

Key Findings

Tres hallazgos: buena parte ya está cubierta por el contrato; algunas funciones solo parcialmente, donde el contrato llega al nivel de infraestructura y ESA espera nivel de aplicación; y quedan sin cubrir los dominios funcionales del Middleware, el almacenamiento S3, la gestión avanzada de incidentes y de cambios y la optimización del rendimiento.

  • Several of the competencies requested by ESA are already fully covered under the contracted infrastructure and managed services scope, particularly those related to Kubernetes cluster lifecycle, security, observability, and DBaaS operations.
  • Some functions requested by ESA are only partially covered, mainly where the contracted scope focuses on infrastructure-level operations, while ESA expectations extend into application- or middleware-level activities.
  • A number of competencies requested by ESA are not included in the contracted scope. These gaps are concentrated around Middleware functional domains, S3 object storage management, advanced incident management, functional change management, and Middleware performance optimization.

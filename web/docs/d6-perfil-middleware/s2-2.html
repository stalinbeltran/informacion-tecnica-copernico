General Scope of the Role

Alcance general en seis puntos: operar a nivel de aplicación, configurar componentes lógicos sobre Kubernetes, diagnosticar incidentes, velar por KaaS/DBaaS/almacenamiento, coordinar con proveedor y ESA, y documentar.

  • Operate the CopernicusLAC Middleware at the application and runtime layer.
  • Configure and maintain logical components on top of Kubernetes (excluding base cluster management).
  • Diagnose functional and technical Middleware incidents.
  • Ensure KaaS, DBaaS, and storage services perform as expected.
  • Coordinate incidents, changes, and improvements with the infrastructure provider and ESA.
  • Document configurations, procedures, and operational events.

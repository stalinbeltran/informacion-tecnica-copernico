Role Objective

Objetivo del rol: operar, configurar lógicamente, supervisar y dar soporte de segundo nivel al Middleware sobre Kubernetes administrado y DBaaS de terceros. No administra infraestructura.

The Middleware Administrator is responsible for the operation, logical configuration, technical supervision, and second-level support of the Copernicus Middleware deployed on managed Kubernetes (KaaS) and third-party DBaaS services. This role does not manage infrastructure, but instead operates and supervises the Middleware runtime and its dependencies, acting as a technical liaison between the platform, the infrastructure provider, and ESA.

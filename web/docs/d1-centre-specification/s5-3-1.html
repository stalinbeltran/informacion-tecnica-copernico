Knowledge Transfer

Transferencia de conocimiento: manuales y documentación técnica, talleres presenciales sobre instalación, configuración y mantenimiento, y acompañamiento durante la fase inicial.

pp. 31

The knowledge transfer plan must ensure that local operators have the necessary knowledge and skills to manage and operate the Platform independently.

  • Objectives:
    • Provide detailed documentation and training materials covering the major aspects of the system.
    • Ensure local operators understand the system architecture, workflows, and maintenance procedures.
    • Facilitate hands-on training sessions to reinforce theoretical knowledge.
  • Activities:
    • Develop comprehensive training manuals and technical documentation.
    • Conduct workshops and training sessions with local operators to cover system installation, configuration, and maintenance.
    • Provide ongoing support and mentorship to local operators during the initial operational phase.
  • Deliverables:
    • Training manuals and technical documentation.
    • Knowledge transfer report detailing the training activities conducted and the proficiency level of local operators.

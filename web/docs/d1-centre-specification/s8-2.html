Annex B - Estimated Man-Power Effort for Operations and Maintenance

Anexo B: estimación indicativa del esfuerzo humano para la fase operativa, expresada en FTE y estructurada por dominios funcionales, asumiendo la infraestructura del Anexo A.

This annex provides an estimate of the man-power resources required to operate and maintain the CopernicusLAC Centre Platform during its operational phase. The figures presented below are indicative and reflect the anticipated effort to ensure reliable service availability, ongoing infrastructure support, user assistance, and compliance with technical, security, and procedural requirements. This effort estimate assumes that the platform infrastructure is aligned with the requirements described in Annex A.

The estimation is structured around the key functional domains involved in the platform’s operation. These include core infrastructure and orchestration, platform services, data validation and user support, security management, and training and documentation. The estimates are expressed in Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) terms and are based on a continuous operational model.

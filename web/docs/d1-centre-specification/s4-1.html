Hardware Specifications

Componentes físicos y virtuales necesarios, con reparto explícito de responsabilidades entre proveedor de infraestructura, desarrolladores y operadores.

2 apartadospp. 22–23

The hardware specifications detail the physical and/or virtual components required to support the operations of the Platform. These components must be capable of handling the data storage, processing, and exchange needs as outlined in the project objectives. The specifications will identify the responsibilities of third-party infrastructure providers, platform developers, and platform operators.

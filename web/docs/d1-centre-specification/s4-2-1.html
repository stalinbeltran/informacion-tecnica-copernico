Operating Systems

Sistemas operativos Linux (por ejemplo Ubuntu) con actualizaciones y parches regulares gestionados por las operaciones de la plataforma.

pp. 23
  • Linux-based operating systems (e.g., Ubuntu) for server environments, chosen for their stability, security, and performance.
  • Regular updates and patches to maintain security and compatibility with other software components.

The platform operations should manage the provisioning and maintenance of the operating systems, including regular updates and security patches. It should also configure the operating systems to meet specific application needs, optimising performance, and ensuring compatibility with other components of the software stack.

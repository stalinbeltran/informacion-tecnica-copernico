Metrics and Targets

99,5 % de disponibilidad anual —unas 44 horas de parada máxima—, mantenimiento en horas valle y objetivo de MTTR inferior a 1 hora.

99,5 %MTTR < 1 h
pp. 32

The system should achieve an uptime of 99.5% annually, translating to a maximum allowable downtime of approximately 44 hours per year. Scheduled maintenance should be planned during off-peak hours with advance communication to minimise user impact. Unscheduled downtime and blocking issues should be addressed promptly, targeting a mean time to repair (MTTR) of less than 1 hour. Continuous monitoring of system health and performance, coupled with automated alerts for potential issues, will ensure rapid response and resolution.

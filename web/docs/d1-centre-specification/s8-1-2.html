Storage Architecture

Almacenamiento por niveles: bloque SSD con 20.000 IOPS y 70 TB, objeto con 2 PB en línea y 20 PB en frío, y cuatro instancias PostgreSQL en HA de 16 vCPU y 64 GB.

2 PB + 20 PB
pp. 38
  • Tiered Storage Strategy combining:
    • Block Storage (SSD): Ultra-fast disks with up to 20,000 IOPS, 1 ms latency, and 70 TB of provisioned capacity for active workflows and cache.
    • Object Storage:
      • 2 PB online tier for near-real-time access to active datasets.
      • 20 PB cold tier for archival of previous years’ Sentinel data.
  • Database Services:
    • PostgreSQL (HA): Four instances (16 vCPU, 64 GB RAM each), ensuring transactional integrity and high availability for catalogue and metadata services.
  • Storage must be distributed and fault-tolerant, capable of serving both interactive access and bulk processing simultaneously.

Server Specifications

Servidores multinúcleo (Intel Xeon Cascade Lake o superior), hasta 512 GB de RAM y 64 CPUs por servidor, mínimo 500 TB de almacenamiento en bruto, y alimentación y refrigeración redundantes.

512 GB64 CPU
pp. 22
  • High-performance servers equipped with multi-core processors (e.g., Intel Xeon Cascade Lake or newer) to handle intensive data processing tasks.
  • Up to 512 GB RAM and 64 CPUs per server to support high-performance computing requirements.
  • Scalable storage solutions, starting with a minimum of 500 TB of raw storage, expandable based on data volume growth. Solutions should include high-speed SSDs for caching and frequently accessed data, and high-capacity HDDs for bulk storage.
  • Redundant power supplies and cooling systems to ensure hardware reliability and uptime.

The infrastructure provider should supply and manage the high-performance servers, scalable storage solutions, and redundant power supplies. Cooling systems should also be maintained by the provider to ensure optimal hardware performance and uptime.

Ensuring that the servers are configured correctly for specific data processing tasks, optimising the use of RAM, CPU and storage, and managing data distribution across storage solutions fall under the platform's responsibilities.

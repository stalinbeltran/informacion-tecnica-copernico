The table below summarizes the estimated man-power required for each activity domain of the CopernicusLAC Centre Platform.

Table 4 - Operations & Maintenance effort summary
Activity DomainEstimated Total Effort
Operations and Infrastructure3.5 FTE (Platform Ops + DevOps)
Support and User Services2.0 FTE (User Support + EO Analyst)
Security and Compliance0.5 FTE
Training and Documentation1.0 FTE (first 12–18 months), then 0.5 FTE ongoing

These estimates assume the use of managed services for Kubernetes and database operations, and rely on cloud-hosted infrastructure with monitoring and backup systems integrated as part of the baseline provisioning. In case of hybrid or fully on-premise deployments, additional system administration resources may be required.

Operational responsibility is expected to transition progressively to local staff during the early phases of maturity, supported by a structured knowledge transfer programme. Adjustments to these figures should be made during project implementation, based on actual platform configuration, usage metrics, and service uptake.

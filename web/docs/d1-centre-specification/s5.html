Operational Specifications

Especificaciones operativas: las dos fases de demostración (pre-operativa desde KO+6 meses y operativa desde KO+12), los requisitos de mantenimiento y el plan de soporte, formación y transferencia de conocimiento a Panamá.

The operational specifications section outlines the activities and requirements necessary to ensure the Platform successful setup, functionality, and long-term sustainability. This section aims to provide a detailed framework for the operational demonstration phases, maintenance protocols, and support and training strategies essential for the effective management and operation of the centre.

A key component of the operational specifications is the knowledge transfer to local operators in Panama. This initiative ensures that local personnel are equipped with the necessary skills and knowledge to manage, operate, and maintain the Platform autonomously. The goal is to build local capacity, foster expertise in EO data management and processing, and ensure the long-term sustainability and self-sufficiency of the centre.

This section is structured to provide a comprehensive guide covering pre-operational (KO+6 months to KO+12 months) and operational demonstration (KO+12 months onwards) phases, maintenance requirements as well as support and training plans. These elements collectively ensure that the Platform is operated efficiently, meets performance expectations, and is prepared for future growth and challenges.

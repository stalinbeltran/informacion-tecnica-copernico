User Roles

Los cinco roles de usuario de la plataforma con sus responsabilidades e interacciones. Una misma persona puede asumir varios roles según la actividad que realice.

RolesDiagramas

This section details the various user roles within the CopernicusLAC platform, outlining their responsibilities and interactions with the system. Understanding these roles is crucial for effective system operation and user support. It is important to note that the same individual may assume multiple roles depending on the specific activity being performed. This flexibility helps in defining the type of services each user needs according to their role at any given time. Users should be encouraged to perform their work within the platform to leverage its capabilities and minimise data downloads.

Each of these roles is integral to the successful operation and utilisation of the CopernicusLAC platform, ensuring that users can effectively access, process, and analyse EO data to meet their specific needs.

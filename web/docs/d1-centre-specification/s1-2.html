Background

Colaboración entre ESA, DG-INTPA y el Gobierno de Panamá para un Centro Regional Copernicus, dentro de la Alianza Digital UE-LAC y la estrategia Global Gateway.

ESAPanamáEU-LAC
pp. 4

The CopernicusLAC initiative is a collaborative effort between the European Space Agency (ESA), the European Commission's Directorate-General for International Partnerships (DG-INTPA), and the Government of Panama. The initiative aims to establish a Regional Copernicus Centre in Panama to facilitate the uptake and utilisation of Copernicus data in the LAC region. The primary focus of the Centre is to support disaster risk reduction and recovery efforts, leveraging EO data and geospatial technology to provide information services to local and regional stakeholders.

This initiative is part of the broader EU-LAC Digital Alliance, which promotes cooperation in digital and space technologies between the European Union (EU) and LAC countries. The CopernicusLAC Centre is envisioned as a pilot project under the EU's Global Gateway strategy, aiming to foster international collaboration, enhance data sharing, and support the development of local capacities in EO data processing and utilisation.

Training Programs

Formación continua: sesiones periódicas sobre nuevas funcionalidades, materiales actualizados y evaluaciones de competencia de los operadores.

pp. 31

Ongoing training programs must ensure that system operators remain proficient in managing and operating the Platform. These programs should include regular training sessions and updates to reflect new features and best practices.

  • Objectives:
    • Keep system operators up-to-date with the latest system updates and best practices.
    • Provide training on new features and functionalities as they are implemented.
    • Enhance the skills and knowledge of system operators to improve overall system performance and reliability.
  • Activities:
    • Schedule regular training sessions and workshops to cover new features and updates.
    • Develop and distribute training materials and guides for new functionalities.
    • Conduct periodic assessments to evaluate the proficiency of system operators and identify areas for improvement.
  • Deliverables:
    • Training schedules and materials.
    • Assessment reports evaluating the proficiency of system operators.

Performance Requirements

99,5 % de disponibilidad con redundancia y recuperación ante desastres, escalado horizontal y automático, despliegue sobre KaaS y DBaaS, baja latencia, y gestión de la dependencia del proveedor.

Performance requirements ensure that the system operates meeting the expected service levels for availability, scalability, and responsiveness.

  • System Availability:
    • Ensure high availability of the system with a target uptime of 99.5%. Implement redundancy and failover mechanisms to minimise downtime and ensure continuous operations.
    • Develop and implement disaster recovery plans and solutions to ensure system resilience and quick recovery in case of failures.
  • Scalability:
    • Design the system to scale horizontally to handle increasing data volumes and user demands. The system should support the efficient management of containerized applications and scale them dynamically based on workload demands.
    • Implement auto-scaling mechanisms to dynamically adjust resources based on current load and performance metrics.
    • The system must be deployable in an infrastructure that provides Kubernetes as a Service (KaaS) and Databases as a Service (DBaaS) relying on the

cloud service provider for the management and orchestration of containerized applications through Kubernetes, and the provision of database setup, maintenance, backups, and scalability through DBaaS.

  • Responsiveness:
    • Optimise the system for low-latency data access and processing. Implement caching strategies and efficient data retrieval mechanisms to enhance system responsiveness.
    • Ensure processing workflows are optimised for performance, minimising execution time and resource usage.
  • Dependency on Resource Provider:
    • Given the reliance on Kubernetes as a Service and Databases as a Service, the system's performance and availability are partly contingent on the reliability and capabilities of the provider.
    • Monitor and manage the provider's scalability capabilities to ensure they meet the system's performance requirements and can allocate resources during peak demand periods.

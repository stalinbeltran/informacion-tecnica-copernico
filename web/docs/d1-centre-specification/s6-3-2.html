Performance Metrics

Tiempo medio de respuesta por debajo de 5 segundos en condiciones normales y 7,5 segundos en picos, con utilización de recursos entre el 50 % y el 70 %.

< 5 s
pp. 33

Maintain an average response time of less than 5 seconds for user requests under normal load conditions, ensuring the system can handle peak loads with response times not exceeding 7,5 seconds. Resource utilisation should be monitored continuously to ensure optimal performance, targeting utilisation levels between 50-70% under normal conditions, allowing headroom for peak loads.

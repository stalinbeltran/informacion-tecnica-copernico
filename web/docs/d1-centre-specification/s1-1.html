D1 · CopernicusLAC Centre Specification and Performance Requirements · Capítulo 1.1

Purpose

Ofrecer una visión completa de las especificaciones y requisitos de rendimiento necesarios para establecer y operar la plataforma del Centro CopernicusLAC.

pp. 4

The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive, high-level overview of the system specifications and performance requirements necessary for the successful establishment and operation of the CopernicusLAC Centre Platform. This document outlines the technical and operational standards that must be met to ensure the Centre's capability to handle large volumes of Earth Observation (EO) data and deliver geospatial information services to stakeholders across the Latin America and Caribbean (LAC) region.

Internal Network:

Red interna de 10 Gbps o superior con rutas redundantes y capacidad de conmutación por error.

pp. 24
  • High-speed internal network (10 Gbps or higher) to ensure efficient data transfer between servers, storage systems, and other hardware components.
  • Redundant networking paths to provide failover capabilities and maintain network availability in case of hardware failure.

The infrastructure provider should maintain the high-speed internal network infrastructure and ensure redundancy and failover capabilities.

The Platform operations should optimise the internal network for specific application needs, monitoring network performance, and managing internal data traffic.

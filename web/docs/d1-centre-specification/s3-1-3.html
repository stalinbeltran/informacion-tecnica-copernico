Data Exchange and Federation Requirements

Mecanismos de intercambio y federación con el Centro de Datos Copernicus de Chile: catálogos visibles, APIs de acceso programático y federación de metadatos sin duplicación.

ChileFederación
pp. 16–17

Effective data exchange and federation are crucial for integrating the Platform with other data centres and platforms, enabling shared access and collaborative use of data.

  • Data Exchange:
    • Implement data exchange mechanisms to facilitate data sharing between the CopernicusLAC Centre Platform in Panama and the Copernicus Data Centre

in Chile. The system should support federation capabilities to enable shared access to data catalogues and retrieval of data across both centres.

  • Ensure data exchange mechanisms are compliant with established standards and support interoperability with other regional and international data centres.
  • Develop APIs to provide programmatic access to data exchange functionalities, allowing integration with third-party platforms and services.
  • Federation:
    • Develop operational interfaces and appropriate tools to federate with other data centres, ensuring visibility of catalogues and access to shared data. The system should support API-based access to its functionalities, allowing integration with third-party platforms and services.
    • Implement mechanisms for metadata and catalog federation to provide a unified view of available data across federated centres without data and metadata duplication.
    • Ensure secure and efficient data transfer protocols to facilitate data federation without compromising data integrity or data access performance.

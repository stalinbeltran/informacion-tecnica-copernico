Roles and Responsibilities

Siete roles descritos: Platform Operator, DevOps Engineer, EO Data Analyst, User Support Officer (bilingüe inglés-español), Security and Compliance Officer, Training Lead y función de Calidad y Documentación.

Roles
pp. 41

The Platform Operator is expected to be the central operational role, overseeing the health of the infrastructure, monitoring container orchestration environments, deploying updates, and coordinating the day-to-day running of services. A continuous effort is foreseen to cover 24/7 availability and ensure proper functioning of the system.

A DevOps Engineer will support automation, monitoring, infrastructure updates, and maintenance of the CI/CD pipelines. This role, essential for managing both preventive and corrective interventions on the platform.

The EO Data Analyst will contribute to the verification and validation of data products, focusing on data quality, consistency of ingestion workflows, and processing outcomes. This effort, while not continuous at full load, is expected to be higher during periods of high ingestion and routine validation operations.

The User Support Officer will ensure helpdesk operations, account management, onboarding support, and resolution of user service requests. A full time person might be considered necessary to address user support in both English and Spanish, ensuring responsiveness across multiple time zones.

For system security and data protection, the involvement of a Security and Compliance Officer is necessary to perform periodic audits, monitor compliance with GDPR and ESA data protection rules, and oversee system hardening. The effort of this role is estimated to be low and based on a quarterly security cycle and audit programme.

A Training and Knowledge Transfer Lead will coordinate the delivery of capacity-building activities aimed at local operators in Panama. This includes preparation of manuals, delivery of initial and refresher training sessions, and mentoring. A dedicated effort is expected during the first 12 to 18 months of operations, with reduced commitment thereafter.

Finally, a Quality Assurance and Documentation function will maintain technical documentation, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and reports for internal and external stakeholders.

D1 · CopernicusLAC Centre Specification and Performance Requirements · Capítulo 1.3

Scope

Alcance geográfico: toda la región LAC, casi 22 millones de km² y más de 670 millones de personas. Alcance operativo: infraestructura, seguridad, métricas, escalabilidad, federación con Chile, usuarios, mantenimiento y transferencia.

pp. 4–5

The scope of this document encompasses both the geographical and operational dimensions of the CopernicusLAC Centre. Geographically, the Centre is designed to serve the entire LAC region, which spans almost 22 million square kilometres and includes more than 670 million people. The Centre's primary focus will be on disaster risk reduction and recovery, with potential expansions into areas such as agriculture and climate resilience.

The CopernicusLAC Centre is a regional facility dedicated to the management, processing, and dissemination of EO data from the Copernicus Program, specifically tailored for the LAC region. The centre aims to provide comprehensive services for data hosting, processing, and access, supporting a wide range of applications, including environmental monitoring, disaster risk reduction, and sustainable development. The centre also focuses on enhancing local capacities by transferring knowledge and providing training to local operators.

The CopernicusLAC Centre Platform refers to the technical infrastructure and software environment that enables the functionalities of the CopernicusLAC Centre. This platform includes the hardware, software, middleware, and network components required to ingest, process, store, and distribute Copernicus EO data. The platform supports various workflows and tools necessary for data management and analysis, ensuring access and usability for different stakeholders. It is designed to be scalable and capable of handling large volumes of data and supporting a growing number of users and applications.

Operationally, the document details the infrastructure requirements, including hardware, software, and network specifications, as well as security protocols to protect data integrity and confidentiality of the platform. It also includes performance metrics to ensure system efficiency, scalability provisions to accommodate growing data volumes and user demands, and a framework for data hosting, processing, and federation with other data centres, particularly the CopernicusLAC Data Centre in Chile. The document also addresses user requirements, outlining the various user profiles and the interfaces necessary to meet their needs, as well as plans for system maintenance, operational support, and knowledge transfer to local operators. This comprehensive approach ensures that the CopernicusLAC Centre can deliver reliable EO data services to support decision-making and strategic planning across the LAC region.

This document serves as the reference for the specifications and performance requirements necessary to establish and operate the CopernicusLAC Centre Platform. It provides detailed guidelines on the infrastructure, software, security, and operational standards that must be met to ensure the Platform's effectiveness and reliability. The specifications outlined in this document will guide the development and implementation phases, ensuring that the Platform meets its objectives and serves the needs of its diverse user base

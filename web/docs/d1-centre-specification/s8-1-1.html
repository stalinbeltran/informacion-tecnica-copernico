Compute & Orchestration Environment

Entorno de cómputo: KaaS y DBaaS obligatorios, clúster Kubernetes en HA escalable a 1000 nodos, y VMs de 4 vCPU/16 GB hasta 32 vCPU/128 GB.

1000 nodos
pp. 38
  • Cloud-Native Design: The platform must be deployed on infrastructure supporting Kubernetes as a Service (KaaS) and Databases as a Service (DBaaS) to allow dynamic workload orchestration and seamless database provisioning.
  • Kubernetes Cluster: A High Availability (HA) managed Kubernetes service capable of scaling to 1000 nodes, supporting:
    • Automated scheduling, auto-scaling, self-healing, and service discovery.
    • Execution of containerized EO workflows (e.g. using CWL/Argo Workflows).
  • Virtual Machines (VMs): Multiple VM classes are required to support both core platform services and high-performance data processing:
  • From 4 vCPU/16 GB to 32 vCPU/128 GB instances.
  • Optimized for parallel, data-intensive EO tasks.

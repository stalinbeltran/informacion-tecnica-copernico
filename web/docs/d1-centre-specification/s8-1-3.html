Network Infrastructure

Red: enlace externo dedicado de 3 Gbps mínimo, fabric interno de 10 Gbps o más con rutas redundantes, e integración con BELLA para conectar con el centro de Chile.

BELLAChile
pp. 39
  • External Connectivity:
    • Minimum 3 Gbps dedicated uplink, protected by multi-layered firewall systems and intrusion detection.
  • Internal Networking:
    • High-performance internal fabric at ≥10 Gbps, supporting rapid data transfer between storage, compute, and platform components.
    • Designed with redundant paths and failover mechanisms to guarantee service continuity.
  • Federated Access & Data Exchange:
    • Integration with the BELLA backbone for interconnection with the Chile Copernicus Data Centre.
    • Catalogue federation and data access through STAC-compliant APIs.

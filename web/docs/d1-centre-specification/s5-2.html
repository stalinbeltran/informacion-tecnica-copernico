Maintenance Requirements

Actualizaciones programadas, mejoras planificadas con mínima interrupción, mantenimiento rutinario con copias y auditorías, y registro documental de toda la actividad.

pp. 30

The maintenance requirements outline the necessary activities to ensure the continuous and efficient operation of the Platform. These activities include scheduled updates, upgrades, and routine maintenance tasks.

  • Scheduled Updates:
    • Regularly update the operating systems, middleware, and application software to ensure security and compatibility with other components.
    • Implement patches and updates for all software components to address known vulnerabilities and improve system performance.
  • Upgrades:
    • Plan and execute system upgrades to enhance hardware and software capabilities as needed.
    • Ensure minimal downtime and disruption during upgrades by scheduling them during off-peak hours and providing advance notice to users.
  • Routine Maintenance:
    • Perform regular backups of all data and system configurations.
    • Monitor system performance and resource utilisation to identify and address potential issues proactively.
    • Conduct periodic security audits and vulnerability assessments to ensure continued compliance with security standards.
  • Documentation:
    • Maintain detailed records of all maintenance activities, including updates, upgrades, and any issues encountered and resolved.
